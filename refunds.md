### 1. Digital Purchases and Final Sales

Sepulchre provides access to premium Discord bot functionality through digitally delivered server licenses.

Each license is purchased through a **one-time payment of $5.99 USD per Discord server**, with no recurring subscription fees.

Once payment has been successfully confirmed, the purchased license is provisioned for the designated Discord server.

Due to the digital nature of these services and their immediate availability, **all completed purchases are generally considered final and non-refundable**, except where refunds or other remedies are required by applicable law.

Our standard policy does not provide discretionary refunds, partial refunds, or account credits for:

- Changes of mind after purchasing a license.
- Accidental purchases where the service has been correctly delivered.
- Failure to use or activate purchased functionality for reasons within the customer's control.
- Dissatisfaction with features accurately described before purchase.
- Decisions to stop operating a Discord server.
- Removal or voluntary deactivation of Sepulchre.
- Purchasing a license without first verifying that the bot meets the intended requirements.

This policy does not exclude mandatory consumer protection rights, including applicable digital-content withdrawal or conformity rights.

### 2. License Transfers as an Alternative to Refunds

Sepulchre provides an authorized license transfer system for customers who wish to relocate their premium access to another Discord server.

Rather than purchasing another license when changing communities, eligible customers may transfer an existing license under the following conditions.

**Transfer Allowance**

- Each server license includes one (1) complimentary transfer per rolling 30-day period.
- Following a completed transfer, a new 30-day waiting period applies before the license can be transferred again.
- Only the authorized license holder may request or initiate a transfer.
- A license can only provide premium access to one Discord server at a time.
- Transferring a license removes premium access from the previously assigned server.
- Completed transfers are final and cannot be automatically reversed.

If a customer requires simultaneous premium access on multiple servers, an additional license must be purchased for each server.

Licenses associated with suspended accounts, fraudulent activity, or confirmed violations of Sepulchre's Terms of Service may be ineligible for transfer.

License transfers are offered as a convenience and do not replace any refund or cancellation rights that may be guaranteed under applicable law.

### 3. Duplicate and Accidental Purchases

Customers are responsible for reviewing their Discord server information and payment details before finalizing a purchase.

If a duplicate license is accidentally purchased for a Discord server that already has an active license, the customer should contact Sepulchre Support with the relevant purchase information.

Where a duplicate payment has been confirmed, our support team may offer to allocate the additional purchase toward activating a different eligible Discord server.

**Duplicate Purchase Conditions**

- The original license must be verifiable through the associated purchase records.
- The additional server must meet Sepulchre's licensing and eligibility requirements.
- The duplicate purchase must not be associated with fraudulent or unauthorized payment activity.
- Customers must provide sufficient information to verify the transaction.
- Reassignment of a duplicate purchase does not grant additional transfers beyond the applicable licensing conditions.

Duplicate purchases are not automatically eligible for discretionary refunds. However, any statutory refund rights or legally required remedies remain unaffected.

Customers are encouraged to report accidental duplicate transactions promptly so that the issue can be reviewed.

### 4. Chargebacks, Payment Disputes, and Fraud Prevention

Sepulchre takes payment security, transaction integrity, and fraudulent purchasing activity seriously.

Customers experiencing billing issues are encouraged to contact our support team so that the matter can be investigated and, where appropriate, resolved.

**Prohibited Payment Abuse**

The following activities may constitute violations of Sepulchre's Terms of Service:

- Knowingly submitting false or fraudulent chargeback claims.
- Attempting to retain access to paid features after intentionally reversing a valid payment.
- Using stolen or unauthorized payment methods.
- Repeatedly exploiting payment disputes to obtain premium licenses without payment.
- Providing deliberately misleading information to payment processors or Sepulchre Support.

**Consequences of Payment Fraud**

Where there is credible evidence of fraudulent or abusive payment activity, Sepulchre may:

- Temporarily suspend affected server licenses pending investigation.
- Revoke licenses associated with confirmed fraudulent transactions.
- Restrict future purchases or transfers involving the responsible account.
- Blacklist accounts or servers involved in serious or repeated payment abuse.
- Submit relevant transaction evidence to the payment processor during a dispute.

Any permanent restrictions will be assessed in accordance with the circumstances and applicable law.

**Legitimate payment disputes, chargeback requests, or the exercise of statutory consumer rights do not automatically constitute misconduct.**

Customers retain the right to contact their bank, card issuer, or payment processor regarding legitimate billing concerns.

### 5. Discord Server Deletion, Account Restrictions, and Enforcement

Purchasing a Sepulchre license does not guarantee the continued availability or ownership of the associated Discord server.

Customers remain responsible for maintaining access to their Discord accounts and ensuring their servers comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Under our standard refund policy, discretionary refunds are not provided when:

- A Discord server is voluntarily deleted or permanently abandoned.
- A server becomes inaccessible following administrative changes or ownership disputes.
- Discord suspends, restricts, or permanently terminates a server.
- A purchasing user's Discord account is disabled, suspended, or banned.
- A server experiences a raid, security breach, or unauthorized administrative actions unrelated to Sepulchre's own conduct.
- The customer loses access to the original server due to circumstances outside Sepulchre's reasonable control.

Eligibility for transferring an existing license will depend on the license status, ownership verification, and the transfer conditions described in Section 2.

Sepulchre is not responsible for reversing enforcement actions or account restrictions imposed independently by Discord.

Nothing in this section removes legally protected remedies where Sepulchre itself fails to provide the purchased service as required by applicable law.
