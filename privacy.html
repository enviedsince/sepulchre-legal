SEPULCHRE — PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

At Sepulchre, we recognize the importance of protecting user privacy and handling information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy describes how Sepulchre collects, processes, stores, protects, and manages information obtained through its Discord bot, associated services, and administrative systems.

Our objective is to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate our features, maintain service reliability, prevent misuse, and provide a secure experience for Discord communities.

By accessing or utilizing Sepulchre, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, personal data is processed in accordance with relevant data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

Sepulchre may collect and process information depending on which features are enabled, how the bot is configured, and how users interact with its services.

1.1 Discord Account and Server Information

To operate its functionality, Sepulchre may process certain information made available through Discord, including:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, and profile information.
  • Discord server IDs, server names, and associated metadata.
  • Channel IDs, role IDs, permission settings, and server structure.
  • Message IDs, timestamps, and other relevant interaction metadata.
  • Server membership events, including joins and departures.
  • Bot command interactions and related operational records.

This information is primarily used to identify authorized users, execute commands, manage permissions, and maintain server-specific functionality.

1.2 Server Configurations and Feature Settings

Sepulchre may store settings and operational information configured by authorized server administrators.

This may include:

  • Moderation rules, automated enforcement settings, and security configurations.
  • Anti-raid protection, honeypot settings, and automated punishment preferences.
  • Ticket panels, support categories, ticket assignments, and transcript settings.
  • Custom embeds, automated messages, welcome systems, and announcements.
  • Server roles, permissions, verification settings, and administrative restrictions.
  • Giveaway configurations, participation records, and winner information.
  • Invite tracking, server activity statistics, and other enabled management features.
  • Feature preferences, automation rules, and module-specific configuration data.

Such information is necessary to preserve server settings and ensure enabled features continue functioning correctly.

1.3 Moderation, Logging, and Security Records

When moderation or security features are enabled, Sepulchre may process relevant activity records, including:

  • Warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, softbans, and other moderation actions.
  • User IDs associated with moderation incidents.
  • Moderation reasons, timestamps, and responsible staff members.
  • Automated moderation triggers and rule violations.
  • Anti-spam, anti-raid, and suspicious activity detection events.
  • Honeypot interactions and their associated enforcement outcomes.
  • Audit records needed to review administrative or automated actions.

These records support accountability, server security, moderation history, and the investigation of possible misuse.

1.4 Messages, Attachments, and Ticket Information

Sepulchre does not require unrestricted storage of every message across every Discord server.

However, certain features may require processing message content or attachments.

Examples include:

  • Ticket messages and generated ticket transcripts.
  • Messages inspected by enabled moderation or filtering systems.
  • Content included in custom embeds, announcements, or automated responses.
  • Message content recorded by configured logging features.
  • Attachments submitted through support or ticket systems.
  • Content explicitly supplied to bot commands requiring message processing.

Message content is processed only where necessary for an enabled feature, requested operation, or legitimate security purpose.

The availability and extent of message-related processing depend on Discord permissions, the bot's implementation, and the configuration chosen by server administrators.

1.5 Licensing and Transaction Information

When purchasing or managing a Sepulchre server license, certain transaction-related information may be collected and retained.

This may include:

  • The Discord user ID associated with the purchase.
  • The Discord server ID associated with the license.
  • License activation and transfer records.
  • Transaction IDs, payment timestamps, and purchase amounts.
  • Payment confirmation and processor reference numbers.
  • License status, restriction history, and relevant billing support records.

Where external payment providers handle checkout, sensitive payment credentials are processed by those providers rather than directly through the Discord bot.

Sepulchre does not need to store full payment card numbers, card security codes, or online banking credentials to manage server licenses.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Information processed by Sepulchre is used to deliver, maintain, secure, and improve the services made available to Discord communities.

Primary Purposes

  • Executing Discord commands and providing requested bot functionality.
  • Maintaining server configurations and administrator preferences.
  • Performing moderation, security monitoring, and automated enforcement actions.
  • Managing tickets, staff workflows, support requests, and transcripts.
  • Operating giveaways, invite tracking, member management, and server automation.
  • Verifying server licenses, processing transfers, and maintaining purchase records.
  • Detecting abuse, fraudulent transactions, and unauthorized activity.
  • Investigating technical issues, command failures, and service interruptions.
  • Maintaining operational reliability and preventing infrastructure misuse.
  • Responding to legitimate support inquiries and legal obligations.

We aim to limit the collection and use of information to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Personal information is not used for unrelated advertising or sold to third parties.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the GDPR or similar data protection legislation applies, Sepulchre processes personal information only where an appropriate legal basis exists.

Depending on the nature of the information and the circumstances, these grounds may include:

Contractual Necessity

Processing required to deliver purchased server licenses, provide contracted services, or fulfill relevant contractual obligations.

Legitimate Interests

Processing reasonably necessary to maintain service security, investigate abuse, prevent fraud, troubleshoot issues, and operate essential bot functions, provided those interests do not override applicable individual rights.

Legal Obligations

Processing required to comply with applicable financial, regulatory, tax, or other legal requirements.

Consent

Processing based on valid consent where consent is legally required and appropriately obtained.

Where Discord server administrators independently determine how certain moderation records, ticket transcripts, or community data are processed, their responsibilities may differ from those of Sepulchre.

The applicable legal basis and controller responsibilities depend on the specific processing activity.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Sepulchre does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Limited information may nevertheless be shared or made accessible where necessary to operate the service.

4.1 Discord Servers and Authorized Staff

Certain information is intentionally accessible to authorized members of the Discord server using the relevant feature.

For example:

  • Moderation records may be visible to authorized moderators.
  • Ticket transcripts may be accessible to permitted support staff.
  • Server activity logs may appear in administrator-designated channels.
  • Giveaway participation and results may be visible within the relevant community.
  • Invite statistics may be displayed to users with appropriate access.

Visibility depends on the features enabled by server administrators and the permissions applied to relevant Discord channels, roles, and commands.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring access to these records appropriately.

4.2 Discord and External Integrations

Sepulchre communicates with Discord's infrastructure to receive events, execute commands, manage server functionality, and deliver responses.

Where optional third-party integrations are supported and enabled, limited information may be transmitted to those services to fulfill the requested functionality.

External services operate under their own privacy policies and data protection practices.

4.3 Payment Processing Providers

Purchases may be processed through external payment platforms.

These providers may collect payment information necessary to complete transactions, verify purchases, prevent payment fraud, and comply with financial regulations.

Sepulchre may receive limited payment confirmation data and transaction references necessary to manage licenses and address billing-related inquiries.

4.4 Infrastructure and Service Providers

Sepulchre may rely on third-party hosting, storage, database, security, or backup providers for essential operations.

Such providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to deliver their services, subject to applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

4.5 Legal and Security Disclosures

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with a valid legal obligation, respond to lawful requests, investigate fraud, or protect the security and rights of affected users.

Any disclosure will be limited to what is appropriate and legally permitted.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Sepulchre aims to retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected.

Retention depends on the type of information, the feature involved, operational requirements, and applicable legal obligations.

5.1 Server Configuration Data

Server-specific configuration data may be maintained while Sepulchre remains installed and authorized within the relevant Discord server.

When Sepulchre is removed, server data may become eligible for deletion in accordance with the service's implemented retention and cleanup procedures.

Some records may require additional retention for security, billing, or legal purposes.

5.2 Moderation and Security Records

Moderation histories, automated security events, and related operational records may be retained while necessary for configured server functionality, incident investigations, or legitimate security requirements.

Authorized administrators may have access to feature-specific controls for clearing stored records where supported.

5.3 Ticket Records and Transcripts

Ticket data and transcripts may be retained according to the configuration and operational requirements of the applicable ticket system.

Deleting or closing a ticket does not necessarily delete a transcript already generated or distributed to authorized staff.

Copies retained independently by Discord server administrators are outside Sepulchre's direct control.

5.4 License and Billing Records

Purchase confirmations, license assignments, transfer histories, and relevant transaction information may be retained while necessary for license verification, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal compliance.

5.5 Backups and Recovery Data

Service information may be included in database backups maintained for disaster recovery and system restoration.

Deleted records may remain temporarily in existing backup copies until those copies expire under the applicable backup retention schedule.

Backups are intended for restoration and continuity purposes rather than ordinary access to deleted information.

Where legally required, deletion requests will also be addressed in accordance with applicable backup and restoration procedures.

6. Data Storage and Infrastructure

Sepulchre relies on controlled application infrastructure and database systems to maintain the information required for its operation.

Operational information may be stored in databases containing server configurations, user identifiers, moderation records, ticket information, and other enabled feature data.

Access to application infrastructure and administrative systems is intended to be restricted to authorized operators and processes.

Depending on the deployment, technical safeguards may include:

  • Restricted access to databases and configuration files.
  • Authentication controls for administrative functions.
  • Protection of application credentials and sensitive tokens.
  • Backup procedures designed to support service recovery.
  • System monitoring and operational maintenance.
  • Separation of data and configuration information between Discord servers where supported.

Sepulchre does not guarantee that all stored information is encrypted in every circumstance unless specifically documented.

Infrastructure providers, storage locations, and relevant international data transfers must be disclosed where applicable under data protection law.

7. User Privacy Rights and Available Controls

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights relating to personal information processed through Sepulchre.

These may include:

  • Right of Access: Request information concerning personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Right to Restriction: Request limitations on certain processing activities.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing carried out on certain legal grounds.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request eligible personal data in a portable format where the legal requirements are met.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent where consent forms the basis for processing, without affecting prior lawful processing.

Server-Level Controls

Authorized server administrators may be able to manage or remove selected information through Sepulchre's available configuration and administrative functions.

Removing the bot or disabling a feature may stop further collection associated with that feature, but it does not necessarily delete all previously stored records.

Privacy and Deletion Requests

Users seeking access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information may contact Sepulchre Support.

Requests may require reasonable identity verification to prevent unauthorized access to another individual's information.

Where another Discord server administrator acts as the responsible data controller, Sepulchre may direct the requester to that administrator or assist with the request as legally appropriate.

8. Security and Data Protection

Sepulchre takes reasonable steps to protect operational information against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, and destruction.

Security measures are designed to reduce the risks associated with database access, administrative credentials, service integrations, and server-specific information.

However, no online platform, communication system, or storage infrastructure can guarantee complete protection against every possible security incident.

Users and administrators are encouraged to maintain secure Discord accounts, review staff permissions, enable available account security features, and avoid exposing confidential information in publicly accessible channels.

Where a personal data breach occurs, applicable notification and response obligations will be followed.

9. Children's Privacy

Sepulchre is designed for use within Discord communities and is not intended to circumvent Discord's minimum age requirements.

Users must meet the minimum age requirements established by Discord and applicable law.

We do not intentionally operate Sepulchre for the purpose of collecting personal information from individuals who are not legally permitted to use Discord.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected in circumstances that violate applicable child privacy requirements, we will take appropriate measures to address the issue.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sepulchre may update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications to bot functionality, data processing practices, infrastructure, third-party integrations, or legal obligations.

The updated policy will identify its effective or last-updated date.

Where changes materially affect how personal information is processed, reasonable notice will be provided when required by applicable law.

Continued use of Sepulchre does not replace any consent or notification requirements that may apply to particular processing activities.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

11. Privacy Inquiries and Contact Information

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, the handling of personal information, data access requests, or deletion inquiries, please contact Sepulchre through our official support community.

Official Support Server: https://discord.gg/88nA6GTD7Q

When submitting a privacy-related request, provide sufficient information for the relevant records to be identified.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information, authentication credentials, payment details, or private server data in publicly accessible channels.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

PRIVACY COMMITMENT

Sepulchre is committed to responsible information handling, appropriate security practices, and transparency regarding the data necessary to operate its Discord services.

We do not sell personal information, and we aim to limit data processing to legitimate operational, security, administrative, and legal purposes.
